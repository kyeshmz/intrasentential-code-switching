4. After placing an English phrase, check what the {language} verb now acts on. If the switch changes it, put the switch on a different phrase.
5. Keep the meaning exact. Preserve timing, quantities, conditions, and what each term refers to. Keep a familiar fixed expression whole instead of splitting it across languages.
6. Match the reader's relationship to the writer. Start with the task, skip filler, and do not invent details or add fake mistakes to sound casual.
7. Before returning the draft, read it as the recipient. Check that the action and conditions are clear and that every sentence has a natural switch. If an English phrase sounds forced, move the switch rather than adding a throwaway phrase.
